Model overview

Each generation repeats one loop: selection (bacteria reproduce according to fitness) → reproduction (weighted random sampling; offspring inherit genotype) → record (resistant frequency).
With the antibiotic present, susceptible bacteria have lower fitness while resistant bacteria survive; without it, resistance carries a small fitness cost.
Because reproduction is random, the same parameters can give slightly different curves. Increase "Run several times" to average out this randomness.
